Core Marxist Economic Definitions

  • Mode of production: The overall way a society organizes production through productive forces and social relations.
  • Forces of production: The means of production (tools, technology, raw materials) together with labor power.
  • Relations of production: Social relationships people enter in production, especially property ownership and class roles.
  • Commodity: A good or service produced for exchange on the market.
  • Labor power: A worker’s capacity to work, sold as a commodity under capitalism.
  • Surplus value: The value created by workers beyond the value of their wages, appropriated by capitalists.
  • Capital: Value that is invested to generate more value through exploitation of wage labor.
  • Class struggle: Conflict between classes with opposing interests, especially labor and capital.
